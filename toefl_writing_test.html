Professor Martinez

This week, we're discussing the impact of artificial intelligence on the job market. Some argue that AI will eliminate millions of jobs, while others believe it will create new opportunities. What is your perspective? Will AI primarily destroy jobs, create jobs, or transform existing roles? Please explain your reasoning with specific examples.

Sarah Chen

I believe AI will primarily destroy jobs, especially in sectors like manufacturing, customer service, and even some white-collar positions like accounting and legal research. We're already seeing self-checkout kiosks replace cashiers, and chatbots handle customer inquiries. The pace of change is too fast for workers to retrain. History shows that technological revolutions always cause temporary unemployment, but this time feels different because AI can learn and adapt.

Marcus Johnson

I disagree with Sarah. AI will create more jobs than it destroys. Every major technological shift—from the Industrial Revolution to the internet—initially caused fear but ultimately led to net job growth. AI will create entirely new categories of work: AI trainers, ethics specialists, human-AI collaboration managers. Plus, by automating routine tasks, AI will free humans to focus on creative, strategic, and interpersonal work that machines cannot replicate. The key is investing in education and retraining programs.
