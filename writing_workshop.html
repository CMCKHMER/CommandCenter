    📋 Writing Templates

    Use these proven structures to organize your writing effectively.

    📖 Story Structure Template

    1. Introduction/Beginning

    Introduce characters, setting, and the main situation.

    Example: "It was a rainy Monday morning when I discovered the mysterious key hidden in my grandmother's attic..."

    2. Rising Action

    Build tension with events leading to the climax.

    Example: "As I tried the key in different locks, strange things began to happen. The lights flickered, and I heard whispers..."

    3. Climax

    The most exciting or turning point of the story.

    Example: "Finally, the key fit! The old chest creaked open, revealing a secret that would change everything..."

    4. Falling Action

    Events after the climax leading to resolution.

    5. Resolution/Ending

    How the story concludes and what we learn.

    📝 Five-Paragraph Essay Template

    Paragraph 1: Introduction
    • Hook: Grab reader's attention
    • Background information
    • Thesis statement (your main argument)
    Paragraph 2: Body Paragraph 1
    • Topic sentence (first supporting point)
    • Evidence/examples
    • Explanation
    Paragraph 3: Body Paragraph 2
    • Topic sentence (second supporting point)
    • Evidence/examples
    • Explanation
    Paragraph 4: Body Paragraph 3
    • Topic sentence (third supporting point)
    • Evidence/examples
    • Explanation
    Paragraph 5: Conclusion
    • Restate thesis (in new words)
    • Summarize main points
    • Final thought/call to action

    🔤 PEEL Paragraph Template

    Perfect for body paragraphs in essays.

    P - Point

    State your main point or topic sentence.

    "Regular exercise is essential for maintaining good health."

    E - Evidence

    Provide facts, examples, or data to support your point.

    "Studies show that 30 minutes of daily exercise reduces heart disease risk by 35%."

    E - Explanation

    Explain how the evidence supports your point.

    "This demonstrates that physical activity strengthens the cardiovascular system and improves overall wellness."

    L - Link

    Link back to the main argument or transition to next paragraph.

    "Therefore, incorporating exercise into daily routines is crucial for long-term health benefits."

      📑 Paragraph Organization

      Learn to write well-organized paragraphs with clear structure.

      📚 Understanding Paragraph Structure

      🎯 Topic Sentence

      The first sentence that tells the main idea of the paragraph.

      "Learning a new language opens many doors."

      📖 Supporting Details

      Facts, examples, and explanations that support the topic.

      "It helps you communicate with more people, understand different cultures, and improve career opportunities."

      ✅ Concluding Sentence

      Wraps up the paragraph and reinforces the main idea.

      "Therefore, language learning is an invaluable skill in today's global world."
